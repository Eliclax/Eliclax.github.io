International IMO Training

The Mountain of Mathematics program combines two of my passions in life: maths Olympiads and teaching. On several occassions I been fortunate enough to combine this with travel, a third passion of mine. This includes lecturing and training the NZ team, as well as Ghana in 2023 and 2026, and Bhutan in 2026. I am always keen to be involved in the training program of other countries, and I believe my experience working with contestants at bronze medal level and below allows me to better help students understand and overcome the challenges they commonly face at that level.

If you represent your country and are interested in involving me in your training program, please contact me. Logistically speaking, I will generally volunteer to train in your national MO program in exchange for the opportunity to visit your country, or to represent your country in some capacity at an international competition. However, neither of these are requirements.
